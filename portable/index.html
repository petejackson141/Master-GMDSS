          Key facts

          Channels 16 and 06
          Every survival craft VHF works on channel 16 (156.800 MHz) and at least one other simplex channel. Channel 06 is the on-scene channel for search and rescue, and for talking to SAR aircraft.
          Power
          At least 0.25 W. A set stronger than 1 W must have a switch to cut it to 1 W or less. Use low power when the other station is close: it saves the battery.
          Range
          VHF is line of sight. From a liferaft, low to the water, a handheld reaches a ship about 5 nm away. The ship, with more power and a higher aerial, can usually be heard much further off. Keep the antenna upright.
          Sealed emergency battery
          A non-rechargeable battery kept for emergencies, sealed so you can see it hasn't been used. It gives 8 hours at full power with a duty cycle of 1:9 and has a shelf life of at least 2 years. Check the expiry date; once the seal is broken, replace it.
          Rechargeable battery
          For tests and routine use, so the sealed battery stays unused. Keep it charged.
          Built for the raft
          Watertight to 1 m for at least 5 minutes, survives a 1 m drop onto a hard surface, works from −20 °C to +55 °C, usable with gloves, and coloured yellow or orange (or marked with a yellow or orange strip).
          Carriage
          SOLAS passenger ships and cargo ships of 500 GT and over carry at least 3; cargo ships of 300–500 GT carry at least 2. Stow them where they can be taken quickly to the survival craft.
          Switching on
          Check three things, in this order: squelch, then channel 16, then HIGH power.
          Squelch
          Turn it down until you hear the hiss, then up until the hiss just stops. Set too high, it cuts out weak stations, often the ones you need.
          Spare battery
          In an exercise or abandon ship, take the sealed spare battery with you. Leave it sealed and fit it only when the radio's own battery runs low.
          Channels 15 and 17
          On-board communication, at 1 W or less. Test with the bridge on these, never on channel 16.
          Talking
          Listen first. Press PTT, pause a moment, speak clearly, release and listen. In distress traffic, start every message with MAYDAY. Keep it short, and end with Over or Out, never both.
